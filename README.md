# Etrian Odyssey Nexus Weapon Damage Comparison

Compares the damage two weapons would do against a given enemy (pretty much the main story/postgame bosses).

In principle this should also work for Etrian Odyssey V since the damage formulas are (to the best of my knowledge) identical, I just didn't load the monsters.

## Data Sources

- Formulas: [Dr. Fetus EO5 Algorithms](https://pastebin.com/jFnMqDET)
- Enemy Stats: [EON Lets Play on Rhematic](https://rhematic.net/eon_lp/enemies_stitched/)
